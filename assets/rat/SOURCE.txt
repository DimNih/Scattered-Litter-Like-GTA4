Rat sound source
=================

The bundled rat_squeak.wav is a short multi-part squeak/chitter remix made
from the CC0 "Squeaky Rat" pack by Iwan "qubodup" Gabovitch:

https://opengameart.org/content/squeaky-rat
https://opengameart.org/users/qubodup

The source page identifies the pack as CC0. The remix uses the Attack, Pain,
and Death vocal variants, converted to mono PCM16 at 22050 Hz for the Android
OpenSL ES buffer player used by this mod.
